It's a brand new year. Have you taken the time to reflect on what you want in 2012?

Almost every day during the holiday break I invested time in the mornings reflecting and journaling on my accomplishments over the last 12 months and what I want out of the next 12. One of the common things among successful people is that we become what we think about. When we invest time thinking, pondering, journaling and setting goals, then we know what direction to take each day.

So what do you want out of 2012? Who do you want to be 12 months from now? If you are struggling with health issues, have you written down the challenges you want to overcome this year? If you can picture in your mind who you want to be then you can create it for yourself. You can choose the thoughts every day that will lead you in the direction you want to go.

However, if you just wake up every morning and do the things that are on your "to do" list without having first invested time in discovering your direction you will wake up 12 months from now realizing you just did a lot of things on your to do list. You will discover that you are really no different than you were 12 months ago. In fact most people will actually be in worse shape because of the natural laws of entropy.
