The most common supplement taken by people are multi vitamins. Are they really essential for good health? In other words, can you be healthy without taking a multi vitamin every day?

The short answer is yes, you can be healthy without taking a multi vitamin every day. But please don't stop reading now because it's likely that you need the long answer and it's probable that taking a multi vitamin is essential to your health.

A multi vitamin is not essential. What is essential are the nutrients that are contained in the multi vitamin. However, our ancestors did not take synthetic multi vitamins. How did they stay healthy? They ate a lot of natural foods that were full of nutrients. They didn't need to supplement.

So again I say, you don't need to take multi vitamins…if you are getting all the nutrients that your body requires according to your genetics for optimal health from the food you eat.
