Essential fatty acids, or EFAs, are fatty acids that humans and other animals must ingest because the body requires them for good health but cannot synthesize them. The term "essential fatty acid" refers to fatty acids required for biological processes, and not those that only act as fuel.

Omega-3 fatty acids are essential fats that also carry with them significant health benefits.

In addition to their role in helping our heart and brains function properly, these fats also help protect our genetic material, therefore reducing damage associated with aging.

So taking fish oil will actually negate early aging and help you live a longer more productive life.
