What is the number one reason to see a Chiropractor? Is it because you have pain? Pain is a good reason but there is one fundamental problem with waiting until something hurts before you see a chiropractor; Pain and problem are not the same thing.

By the time you are experiencing pain you typically have had a problem brewing for months, years or even decades. Unfortunately if a problem has been developing for that long you are usually on the path to much bigger issues including permanent arthritis. This will make your problem and your pain even worse down the road.

Living in our world of sitting, texting and being weekend warriors can cause small problems in our spine. These small problems, that will turn into big problems if they are neglected, will eventually interfere with your quality of life and your ability to do the things that you love. One of the reasons this happens is because every joint in the spine must move freely in order to maintain proper proprioception, a necessary nerve stimulus to your brain that helps everything in your body work correctly. This decreased brain stimulus will cause a downward spiral that will eventually lead to pain in addition to many more serious health conditions. If every joint in your spine is not moving freely it is imperative that you get it moving.

This might require the help of a chiropractor.
