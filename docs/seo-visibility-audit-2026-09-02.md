## Indexing and technical

### GSC sitemap: 1,041 URLs submitted, 0 marked indexed

The live sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` contains 1,041 `<loc>` tags and was last downloaded by Google on **29 Aug 2026**. URL Inspection still shows the homepage, schedule, booking, contact, services, intake, and several local pages as “Submitted and indexed.” Google has not associated those indexed URLs with this sitemap yet — typical after a WordPress → Next.js cutover, and a sign that most programmatic URLs are not indexed.

**URL Inspection (10 URLs): 9 pass, 1 fail.** Homepage last crawled 1 Sep 2026 as mobile. HTTPS canonical matches. Robots allowed.

- `/neuropathy/` is “Not indexed: Not found (404)” and does not exist in the Next.js app.
- Legacy `sitemap_index.xml` (submitted 27 May 2022, last downloaded 16 Jun 2025) still sits in GSC with **1 error and 3 warnings**. Remove it.

### Ignore the DataForSEO 0/100 on-page score

DataForSEO flagged HTTP, missing canonical, missing OG, and missing schema on every audited page. **Live HTML contradicts that:** HTTPS + HSTS on Vercel, `rel=canonical`, full Open Graph (1200×630 image), MedicalBusiness JSON-LD, GA4, and GSC verification. Treat that 0/100 as a crawler parse failure, not a production outage.

**Real on-page gaps:**

- Homepage title does not include “chiropractor Rio Rancho”
- Schema is thin (no geo, hours, sameAs, or aggregateRating)
- H1 is brand-first

## What to fix first

1. **Win the map pack — this is the local click leak.** Absolute Chiropractic sits at Maps #1 with 512 reviews. Synergy is ~#7. GBP categories, photos, weekly posts, NAP match to the website, and a post-visit review ask will move more new-patient traffic than another programmatic city page.

2. **Make the homepage a Rio Rancho chiropractor page.** Title, H1, and MedicalBusiness schema should name the city, specialty, phone, geo, hours, and rating. GSC already sends 709 impressions for “chiropractor rio rancho” to this URL — the snippet and schema are not earning the click (3.7% CTR at position 12).

3. **Clean GSC sitemaps and the 404.** Delete `sitemap_index.xml`. Keep `/sitemap.xml`. 301 `/neuropathy/` to the matching service or condition URL (408 lingering impressions). Request indexing on the hub pages after the title/schema change.

4. **Connect intake and measure conversions.** Either embed the real HighLevel/Atlas intake on `/new-folks/intake-forms/` or stop telling patients the form is ready. Add FormSubmit `_next` + captcha. Fire `generate_lead` from contact, GHL booking, and calls (505-891-2280).

5. **Stop feeding thin programmatic URLs.** August’s impression spike with a 0.92% CTR is the sitemap of ~1,000 city×service pages being discovered. Tighten internal linking to Rio Rancho hubs, noindex or consolidate the weakest combinations, and put effort into unique condition pages that already almost rank (nerve chart, decompression, SoftWave).
